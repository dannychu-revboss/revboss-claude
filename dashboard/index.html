What changed & why

Anil felt the original read as salesy and wanted the frame shifted from the value of 'applying for a job' toward 'applying skills' — proving fit/worthiness by revealing human capability and purpose — plus the idea that organizations should signal to candidates who don't get a callback that they are more than a resume. Ambiguous/subjective ask, so this is a best-effort rewrite: (1) dropped the 'leave you with... a career' promise that read as the most salesy line and reopened on the applicant's real problem (a resume dropped into a void); (2) reframed the core value prop around applying skills to prove fit and reveal human capability + purpose; (3) reworked the bullet list from outcomes ('build networks') toward revealing capability a resume misses; (4) added a stanza on organizations signaling to everyone who takes part — even non-callback candidates — that they are more than a resume; (5) softened the company/participant close and swapped the CTA question to match the new resume theme. Preserved voice, emojis (🔹, •), line breaks, and the @[Innovation Challenge®](urn:li:organization:11786165) mention syntax exactly. The attached video asset was not changed. Alternative if Anil prefers less rewrite: keep the original opening two lines and only add the 'more than a resume' stanza.
